This is a **sample note** for the notebook. It explores a small idea: a useful workspace does not need to show everything at once.

## Keep what matters

A document, a terminal, and a short list of questions can be enough for a focused session. Everything else can wait until it becomes relevant.

- Write down the next concrete task.
- Keep reference material within reach.
- Leave a short note before stopping.

> A good stopping point makes the next beginning easier.

## A question to keep

Which part of the workspace helps the work, and which part simply occupies attention?
