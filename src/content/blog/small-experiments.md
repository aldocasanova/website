This is a **sample research note**. The structure below is a template for an experiment, not a claim about measured results.

## Ask one question

What changes when one variable changes? Write the question before collecting observations, and keep the conditions of the comparison explicit.

| Part | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Question | The behavior being investigated |
| Baseline | The starting configuration |
| Change | One deliberate modification |
| Observation | What happened during the run |
| Limitation | What the comparison cannot establish |

## Leave room for uncertainty

An unexpected observation can suggest another question without proving an explanation. Keeping those two things separate makes a notebook easier to revisit.
